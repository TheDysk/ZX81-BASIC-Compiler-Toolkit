10 REM CLOCK FACE - FLOATING POINT DEMO
20 REM SIN, COS AND PI ARE WORKED OUT BY THE ZX81 ROM. VARIABLES HOLD WHOLE
30 REM NUMBERS, SO THE ANGLES ARE WORKED OUT INSIDE EACH EXPRESSION.
40 CLS
50 FOR M=0 TO 59
60 PLOT 32+20*SIN (M*PI/30),22+20*COS (M*PI/30)
70 NEXT M
80 FOR H=1 TO 12
90 PRINT AT 11-INT (8.5*COS (H*PI/6)+0.5),15+INT (8.5*SIN (H*PI/6)+0.5);H
100 NEXT H
110 LET T=10*60+10
120 FOR R=0 TO 10
130 PLOT 32+R*SIN (T/720.0*2*PI),22+R*COS (T/720.0*2*PI)
140 NEXT R
150 FOR R=0 TO 16
160 PLOT 32+R*SIN ((T-INT (T/60)*60)/60.0*2*PI),22+R*COS ((T-INT (T/60)*60)/60.0*2*PI)
170 NEXT R
180 PRINT AT 21,0;"10:10   PI=";PI
